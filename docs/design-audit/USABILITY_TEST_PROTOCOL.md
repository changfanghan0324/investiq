# Five-second and task-test protocol

No human participant results are claimed. This document is the protocol and
recording template for the owner to run with at least five people, including at
least three people without a finance or data background.

## Five-second test

Show only the home page for five seconds, then hide it. Ask:

1. What does this site do?
2. What would you click or type first?
3. Which data is real?
4. Which data is demonstration data?

Success criteria:

- At least 4/5 identify company research or investment analysis.
- At least 4/5 identify the ticker search as the first step.
- 5/5 do not mistake synthetic market history for real market history.

## Task test

Ask each participant to complete, without coaching:

1. Search AAPL.
2. Find revenue.
3. Open valuation.
4. Compare AAPL and MSFT.
5. Build a two-stock portfolio.
6. Run the DCA public demo.

## Moderator notes

- Do not explain SEC, synthetic data, risk-free rate, or DCF before the test.
- Stop timing only when the participant states the task is complete.
- Record the participant's words, not an interpretation.
- Treat an accidental Advanced disclosure as a misclick only when the
  participant says it was not their intended next step.
- After all tasks, ask what felt trustworthy and what felt confusing.
