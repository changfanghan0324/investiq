# InvestIQ product history

InvestIQ began as an evidence-first investment research and portfolio analytics demo. Its
public demo included SEC company research and synthetic market examples. The synthetic series
were later disclosed explicitly, but the market-oriented surface still created avoidable
ambiguity for a public portfolio.

The ModelGuard migration keeps the repository, deployment project, and source history intact
while retiring the market, portfolio, DCA, company, and assistant surfaces from the active
runtime. The old implementation remains recoverable at the archive tag
`investiq-v1-before-modelguard`.
