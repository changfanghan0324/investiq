# AI-assisted development disclosure

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

- **Codex:** repository audit, implementation, integration, deterministic tests, QA, documentation,
  and delivery evidence.
- **Claude Opus:** independent product, finance, data-governance, accessibility, and recruiter-
  readability review before major phases, including explicit objections and disagreement rounds.
- **Fang Han Chang / Peter Chang:** product positioning, acceptable financial definitions,
  public-profile approval, licensing, merge, and release decisions.

AI output is not treated as financial evidence. Official source receipts establish reported facts.
Pure deterministic code performs financial calculations, and tests pin definitions and numerical
results. Optional AI explanations receive calculated summaries and cannot change calculations. AI
does not supply investment recommendations, licensed prices, participant results, or owner contact
details. Review records are retained in `docs/audit/`.
